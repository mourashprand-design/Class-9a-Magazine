ORCHID + MACAW

Say hello to Orara!

Our class mascot, Orara, brings together two colourful symbols: the orchid and the macaw. The orchid represents growth, beauty and individuality, while the macaw brings colour, confidence and a lively spirit.

Together, they represent a class where different personalities and talents can grow side by side.

What Orara stands for

Creativity: Every student has their own way to express an idea.

Growth: We learn from challenges and from each other.

Togetherness: Our differences make our class more interesting.
