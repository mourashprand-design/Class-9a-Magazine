WHO WE ARE

About the magazine

A small digital home for the big ideas, talents and memories of Class 9A.

Created by our class, for everyone.

This magazine is a place where students can share what they create and what matters to them. It brings together writing, art, photographs, school activities and the people who make our class unique.

Every article, poem and photograph gives us a chance to see our classmates from a new perspective. We hope these pages capture a little piece of our school year.

Our aim

To celebrate student creativity, encourage everyone to express themselves and keep our class memories in one place.
